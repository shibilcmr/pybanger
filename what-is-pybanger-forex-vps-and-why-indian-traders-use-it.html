What is a Forex VPS

A Forex VPS is a virtual private server that runs your trading terminal non stop. Instead of running MT4 or MT5 on your home computer, you run it on a server in a data centre.

This means your trades keep running even when:

  • Your home internet disconnects
  • Your laptop restarts
  • There is a power cut at your house
  • You are travelling or sleeping

For any trader running Expert Advisors or automated strategies, a VPS is not optional. It is required.
