What Matters When Choosing a Forex VPS in India

If you are an Indian trader, here is what you should actually look at before paying for a VPS.

Payment method. UPI is the easiest way to pay for a VPS from India. No forex card needed, no conversion fees. Providers like pybanger, VCCLHosting and YouStable accept UPI [citation:3][citation:11][citation:8]. Providers like ForexVPS and DigitalOcean do not.

Pricing currency. INR pricing means you know exactly what you are paying. USD pricing means your cost changes with the exchange rate and you pay conversion fees every month.

Server location. Pick the location closest to your broker. Indian brokers use Mumbai servers. Most forex brokers use London, New York or Singapore servers. If your broker is in India, choose a Mumbai based VPS from Webyne, Hapih Host or VCCLHosting. If your broker is in London, choose a London or Bulgaria based VPS from pybanger.

RAM and CPU. For 2 to 3 MT5 terminals, 4GB RAM and 2 core CPU is enough. For 5 or more terminals, go with 8GB RAM and 4 cores [citation:1].

Storage type. NVMe SSD is much faster than old SATA or standard SSD drives. This affects how quickly your EAs process ticks and place orders [citation:4]. Hapih Host uses standard SSD on cheaper plans, while pybanger, Webyne and VCCLHosting use NVMe on all plans.

Support time zone. If your provider's support is in US or EU hours, you get replies when you are asleep. Indian time zone support from pybanger, Webyne and VCCLHosting is a big advantage.

Setup time. A VPS should be ready within an hour of payment. pybanger delivers within one hour. Webyne and VCCLHosting also have fast setup. If a provider takes days, look elsewhere.
