About pybanger

pybanger is a Forex VPS provider based in Kerala, India. We started this service because we saw traders struggling with slow, unreliable VPS that caused missed trades and frustrating latency during volatile market hours. Our goal is simple: give Indian forex traders fast, affordable VPS infrastructure that just works.

We resell high-performance VPS from trusted upstream data centers across 6 global locations, optimized for MT4 and MT5 terminals. Whether you run one terminal or eight, our plans are built to keep your bots and EAs running 24/7 without interruption.
