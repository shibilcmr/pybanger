What Matters When Choosing a Forex VPS in India

If you are an Indian trader, here is what you should actually look at before paying for a VPS.

Payment method. UPI is the easiest way to pay for a VPS from India. No forex card needed, no conversion fees. Providers that accept UPI are easier to work with month to month.

Pricing currency. INR pricing means you know exactly what you are paying. USD pricing means your cost changes with the exchange rate and you pay conversion fees every month.

Server location. Pick the location closest to your broker. Most forex brokers are in London, New York or Singapore. Bulgaria is a good budget option for London based brokers because latency is very close.

RAM and CPU. For 2 to 3 MT5 terminals, 4GB RAM and 2 core CPU is enough. For 5 or more terminals, go with 8GB RAM and 4 cores.

Storage type. NVMe SSD is much faster than old SATA drives. This affects how quickly your EAs process ticks and place orders.

Support time zone. If your provider's support is in US or EU hours, you get replies when you are asleep. Indian time zone support is a big advantage.

Setup time. A VPS should be ready within an hour of payment. If a provider takes days, look elsewhere.
